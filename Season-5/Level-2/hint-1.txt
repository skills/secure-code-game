Every route starts by working out who is calling. Not one of them finishes the
thought.

Go through the three endpoints and, for each, ask two separate questions:

  1. Who is making this request?
  2. Is that person allowed to do this, to this particular report?

The code answers the first question every time. Count how often it answers the
second one.

Then look again at where each fact comes from. Some of what the code treats as
established truth arrives in the request itself.
